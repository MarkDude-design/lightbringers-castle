# Constraints

## Curated Bots
- Must be safe
- Must be predictable
- Must have 5–10 responses
- Must follow Castle tone

## Experimental Bots
- Can be weird
- Can be surreal
- Can misfire
- Must stay sandboxed
- Must not interfere with curated bots

## Early Models
- Must be wrapped
- Must be constrained
- Must be supervised
- Must be veto-able
